# Ten shop illustrations

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, one image per design. These ten distinct designs are assigned deterministically across the 87 fictional ABC shops. Robot characters and event parcels are animated SVG overlays; no image API is called during play.

Optimized WebP assets are saved in `public/shops/`, preserving each generated composition.

## city-market

Saved asset: `public/shops/city-market.webp`.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isometric shop backdrop for the ABC Shop retail game.
Create a beautiful cute simple isometric cutaway supermarket diorama, landscape 3:2 composition. Muted forest green, sage, cream and dusty pastel palette, hand-painted cut-paper look, subtle paper grain, gentle soft shadows, cozy rounded objects. Whole store fully visible, fills about 85% of image, isolated on a flat warm cream #f0ead4 background. Isometric diamond floor with far corner near upper-center, two back walls, NO front walls or roof. Shelving grouped along left and middle-left. Glass fridge against the back-right wall. Checkout counter on right/front-right. Large unobstructed aisle across the lower-middle and center, for overlaying moving robots. Tiny safety camera on back wall. Simple products without readable labels. No people, no robots, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Keep scene readable at small size, few large objects. Distinct shop design: a Paris neighborhood market, sage painted walls, arched back windows, dark green awning, fresh fruit crates.
